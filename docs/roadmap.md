# Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction, not a delivery promise. Priorities may change as device testing and contributor feedback arrive.

## Current prototype

- Manual gear selector with reverse and gears 1–5
- Independent clutch, brake, and throttle input
- RPM, speed, engine-state, and shift-feedback simulation
- Optional FMOD RPM bridge

## Next: MVP hardening

- Complete device validation across small, standard, and large iOS and Android screens
- Improve landscape layout and simultaneous-touch reliability
- Add clear guidance for starts, clutch bite point, and basic upshifts
- Add haptic and visual feedback for stalls, jerks, and rejected shifts
- Replace temporary audio content with original, redistributable sound content

## Later exploration

- Multiple fictional vehicle feel profiles
- Configurable realism and accessibility settings
- Practice scenarios such as hill starts
- Community-created profiles or sound packs, subject to licensing and moderation rules

## Out of scope

- Competitive racing, lap times, and leaderboards as the primary experience
- Unlicensed use of vehicle manufacturer branding
